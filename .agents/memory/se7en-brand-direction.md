The client wants the storefront to take direction from the supplied SE7EN logo because it feels Gothic. The user selected a black background with brighter red lettering for the logo, asked for the Gothic direction to carry across the full storefront, and chose a refined editorial treatment over stronger Old English/blackletter headings.

**Why:** The user approved an editorial Gothic streetwear look for this client's storefront.

**How to apply:** Keep a consistent dark, red-accented Gothic identity across the storefront, using editorial display headings and the logo as visual anchors while preserving readability of products and shopping controls.
