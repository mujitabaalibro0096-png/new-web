name: SE7EN client visual direction
description: Client's desired logo-led Gothic streetwear treatment.
---

The client wants the storefront to take direction from the supplied SE7EN logo because it feels Gothic. The user selected a black background with brighter red lettering for the logo and asked for the Gothic direction to carry across the full storefront.

**Why:** This direction was explicitly provided and approved for the client site.

**How to apply:** Keep a consistent dark, red-accented Gothic identity across the storefront, using the logo as the visual anchor while preserving readability of products and shopping controls.
