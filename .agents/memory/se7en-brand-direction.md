The client wants the storefront to take direction from the supplied SE7EN logo because it feels Gothic. The user selected a black background with brighter red lettering for the logo.

**Why:** This direction was explicitly provided and approved for the client site.

**How to apply:** Keep the storefront dark and red-accented, using the logo as the visual anchor while preserving readability of products and shopping controls.
